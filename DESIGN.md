# DESIGN.md — The Bookable Payable

## 1. What did we eventually understand about these documents that we did not understand on day one?

On day one, the problem looked like a standard document-extraction pipeline: run a Vision-LLM over each PDF, copy the printed fields into `AUTODRAFT_SCHEMA.md`, and match strings against `master_data/`. That initial mental model failed as soon as we tested our first extractions against `erp.py`.

We realized that **an invoice PDF is formatted for human comprehension, whereas `erp.py` is a strict bottom-up calculator**. Invoices routinely print redundant summaries that a human knows not to add twice, but that `erp.py` will sum blindly if both are populated:
- **Tax Placement & Rounding Bases:** Many invoices print tax rates on each line item and also display a tax breakdown box at the bottom (`DU-06.pdf`, `INV-13.pdf`, `INV-27.pdf`). Populating both `line_items[].taxes[]` and header `taxes[]` causes `erp.py` to charge tax twice. Furthermore, where a tax sits changes how `erp.py` rounds it: in `INV-11.pdf`, four freight lines taxed at 24% sum to `16.11 EUR` when rounded per line (`83.21 EUR` gross), whereas applying 24% once at the header (`67.10 * 24%`) rounds to `16.10 EUR`. Conversely, in `DU-06.pdf`, four lines at 6% IVA round to `5.80 EUR` individually, but the document's tax summary computes 6% on the grouped base (`96.52 * 6% = 5.79 EUR`).
- **Compound Levies & Withholding Taxes:** In `INV-19.pdf` (Ghana), NHIL (2.5%), GETFund (2.5%), and COVID-19 (1%) levies apply to the net subtotal (`6,600.00 GHS`), while the 15% VAT (`1,049.40 GHS`) is calculated on top of the levy-inclusive subtotal (`6,996.00 GHS`). Because `erp.py` derives rate-only header taxes from the plain `net_base`, leaving `tax_amount` blank understates the VAT (`990.00` vs `1,049.40`); passing the printed `tax_amount` preserves the exact compound levy. Similarly, in `HLD-01.pdf` (Thailand), the 3% withholding tax (`235.44 THB`) reduces the payment due and must be represented with a negative `tax_amount` (`"-235.44"`).
- **Packet Noise:** Many PDFs bundle an invoice with supporting customs declarations (`INV-09.pdf`), underlying utility bills (`INV-31.pdf`), shipment logs (`INV-28.pdf`, `HLD-10.pdf`), `Original`/`Duplicado` copies (`INV-25.pdf`), or non-payable reminders (`DU-08.pdf`), estimates (`INV-23.pdf`), and delivery notes (`DU-05s.pdf`).

The shift was treating the task as **accounting decomposition**: translating visual layouts into non-overlapping components that satisfy `erp.py`'s arithmetic.

## 2. When our system meets a document unlike any it has seen, what does it actually do — and why does that generalise instead of guessing?

Rather than writing supplier-specific rules, we built a **two-stage verification loop (`src/extractor.py` + `src/reconciler.py`)** paired with an **indexed master-data resolver (`src/resolver.py`)**:

1. **Iterative ERP Verification Loop:** On Turn 1, the PDF is processed using a fast extraction configuration (`thinking_budget=0`) to segment the packet and produce an initial JSON draft. Before accepting the draft, `src/reconciler.py` evaluates each payable through `erp.py`'s internal functions (`_line_base`, `_line_taxes`, `_header_taxes`, `erp_book`).
2. **Diagnostic Feedback Without Target Plugging:** If a line extension diverges from `_line_base`, if both line and header taxes are populated simultaneously, or if the recomputed gross leaves a residual, `generate_diagnostic_signals` produces structured observations describing how `erp.py` evaluated each bucket. Only when a discrepancy is detected does the system escalate to a second turn with deeper reasoning enabled (`thinking_budget=2048`) inside the same conversation context.
3. **Verifying Before Mutating:** Any adjustment must be supported by an independent check on the page. For instance, `DU-05.pdf` lists `100 PC` at `771.66` with `PU: 100` and line total `771.66` (price per 100 units), whereas `HLD-08.pdf` lists `Quantity: 468.0`, `Unit: 1000`, `Unit Price: 276.74` and line total `129,514.32` (where `468 * 276.74` already equals `129,514.32`). Because `reconciler.py` checks whether `quantity * unit_price` already matches the printed line total before flagging a line-extension mismatch, `HLD-08.pdf` books cleanly on Turn 1 without interference.
4. **Scalable Master-Data Resolution:** `src/resolver.py` pre-indexes exact identifiers (normalized VAT IDs, IBANs, emails, PO numbers) in $O(1)$ hash dictionaries, falls back to weighted fuzzy token matching on legal names and addresses, derives payment terms from `(due_date - invoice_date)` when text aliases are absent, and leaves codes blank (`""`) when confidence falls below threshold.

## 3. Was there a document you concluded could not be solved the way the others were? If so, which, and how did you know?

Yes. Several documents in the dataset contain structural gaps where the figures printed on the page cannot deterministically reconcile to the stated total under `erp.py`'s net-price formula without inventing ungrounded numbers:

1. **`HLD-05.pdf` (Embedded Alcohol Excise Duty `IEC` + Non-Taxable Deposit `SDR`):** On this Portuguese beverage invoice, each line carries a promotional percentage discount (`Desc. Prom.: 1,049.60 EUR` on `Ilíquido: 1,796.54 EUR`, leaving `746.94 EUR`) alongside a per-line alcohol excise duty (`Total Valor IEC: 88.33 EUR`, annotated `"*IEC já liquidado através de DIC Global"`) and bottle deposit lines (`Depósito SDR: 25.80 EUR`, exempt from VAT). The document's VAT base (`Base de Incidência: 835.27 EUR`) embeds the `88.33 EUR` excise duty (`746.94 + 88.33 = 835.27`), even though `erp.py` only applies line percentage discounts to `quantity * unit_price`. Rather than fabricating ungrounded unit prices or a plug discount to force the gross (`1,126.27 EUR` vs `1,037.94 EUR`), our solver preserves the grounded components from the page.
2. **`INV-06.pdf` and `INV-25.pdf` (Tax-Inclusive Unit Prices Only):** Both `INV-06.pdf` (`Unit Price (incl vat)`, `1,683.98 ZAR` gross with `169.83 ZAR` VAT) and `INV-25.pdf` (`P.VENDA C/IVA`, `176.00 EUR` gross with `32.91 EUR` IVA) print only tax-inclusive unit prices on their line items, and `INV-06.pdf` additionally omits the zero-rated tax marker on `Clover Uht Full Cream Long Life` (`199.98 ZAR`). Because net unit prices do not appear on the page, emitting the printed unit prices alongside the printed tax causes `erp.py` to add the tax on top of the gross price (`1,853.81 ZAR` and `208.91 EUR`) rather than inventing unprinted net prices.
3. **`INV-26.pdf` (Truncated Table / Missing Rows 29–31):** On Page 1, the item numbering jumps directly from Item `28` to Item `32`, leaving a `53.80 MYR` gap between the 32 visible lines (`819.73 MYR`) and the printed total (`873.53 MYR`), alongside a `Quotation No.` header label that caused our triage pass to decline the document rather than fabricate the missing lines.
